## 2. What does he teach about "rolki zasięgowe" versus expert or sales reels?

### Takeaway
I found **no source** in which Gajos uses the term "rolki zasięgowe" or sets out a reel taxonomy (zasięgowe / eksperckie / sprzedażowe), ratios, a hook framework or a posting cadence. The only documented statements about short-form from him are in the user's internal notes, and they are dismissive in SEO terms: Shorts are "a separate world" where SEO doesn't matter, and Shorts have no effect on long-form recommendations. Some of the taxonomy the brief asked about does exist in Polish marketing, but it belongs to other people (e.g. Sprawny Marketing), not Gajos.

### Cited Findings
- Internal notes, Gajos's position:
  - "Nie ma wpływu: ruch z reklam, Shorts, monetyzacja (Bartek ma tu znak zapytania), polubienia i komentarze (znikomy), subskrypcje."
  - "Shorts: SEO nie ma znaczenia, osobny świat — dobra forma na krótkie tematy."
  - Source: internal `zasady-seo-yt.md`, sections 1 and 10 (not public)
- Internal notes, his other relevant principles that carry over to hooks:
  - The viewer path is "słowa kluczowe → miniatura → tytuł → zajawka → wstęp".
  - "Pierwsze 30–40 sekund są krytyczne. Odrzut powyżej ok. 50%… = źle trafiony widz albo zła zajawka".
  - A teaser built on "najbardziej „przypałowa" historia z realizacji zakończona pytaniem („a wiecie, że dało się tego uniknąć?")" works better than "w tym odcinku dowiecie się…".
  - "YouTube to medium rozrywkowe; bardzo techniczne treści trafiają do wąskiej grupy."
  - Source: internal `zasady-seo-yt.md` (not public)
- Internal notes, healthy-channel benchmark: about 80% of views from the home page and suggested videos. Traffic from LinkedIn and Instagram is a "novelty effect" that is unstable. — internal `zasady-seo-yt.md`
- For contrast only (not Gajos): Sprawny Marketing's reels training splits reels into four goals. These are reach ("rolki na zasięg"), community engagement, expert positioning, and sales. It notes that talking-to-camera reels are needed for an expert image. — [sprawnymarketing.pl/szkolenia/rolki](https://sprawnymarketing.pl/szkolenia/rolki/)
- For contrast only: the PARP-listed course "Magia Reelsów" covers educational, storytelling, expert and sales reels. — [uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl](https://uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl/wyszukiwarka/uslugi/podglad?id=3228097)
- For contrast only: Shoper presents reels as top-of-funnel attention, with sales coming from later steps, and suggests 5–15 s for sales and viral reels. — [shoper.pl](https://www.shoper.pl/learn/artykul/rozkrec-swoje-zasiegi-wykorzystaj-rolki-na-instagramie-w-e-biznesie)

### Inferences
- If the report needs "the Gajos approach to reach reels", it has to be **reconstructed from what his channel does**, not from his stated teaching (see section 3). The honest framing is: "Gajos treats Shorts as a separate, non-SEO reach channel; his own channel uses Shorts as clipped hooks from long episodes."
- His long-form hook rules ("przypałowa" story plus a question, delivering the promise fast) are the closest documented material to hook guidance. Applying them to reels is an extrapolation.

### Gaps
- No named framework, ratio, cadence, humour guidance or "zasięgowe vs sprzedażowe" distinction attributable to Gajos.
- No statement found on how Shorts feed long-form or sales, beyond "Shorts don't affect long-form".

### Inferences (my analysis; not verified frame-by-frame because playback metadata was blocked)
- **Format:** these are talking-head clips cut from Polihistor episodes, each a self-contained answer to one question. The title is the hook, posed as a curiosity question that ordinary viewers already wonder about, for example "Ile pieniędzy dostają…", "Ile Rosja zarabia dziennie…". The working mechanics are a provocative or paradoxical claim ("Putin zna się na rynku energetycznym?", "Stalin wrócił na stację metra"), timeliness (Istanbul talks, Merkel on Nord Stream), and recognisable villains or names (Putin, Zełenski, Merkel).
- **Humour:** the titles show no evident humour device. The pull comes from news hooks and gentle irony ("Rosja nagle dba o prawo międzynarodowe"), not jokes. This counts against the brief's assumption of humour-led reach reels.
- **Connection to long-form:** the Shorts work as trailers for the hosts and the series (the #polihistor hashtag), not as SEO assets. That is consistent with Gajos's stated view that "Shorts… osobny świat" and do not affect long-form recommendations.
- **Ceiling:** even at 783 Shorts, only about 10% exceed 10k. Expert-clip Shorts in a "serious" niche give steady low reach with occasional news-driven spikes. This is a useful benchmark for B2B or "boring" niches.
